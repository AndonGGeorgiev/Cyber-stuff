How to Scrub Your Name from Data Broker Sites in 20 Minutes (Without Paying a Dime)

Have you ever googled your own name and felt a chill run down your spine?

Within seconds, you might have found your exact home address, phone number, age, names of your relatives, and even your approximate income—all displayed openly on websites you’ve never heard of.

You didn’t publish this information. Data brokers did.

These hidden companies scrape public records, voter registration lists, and purchase histories to build digital dossiers on you. They then sell this data to marketers, scammers, or anyone with $5 and a credit card.

While premium services will charge you hundreds of dollars a year to remove this information, you can do it yourself, for free, in under 20 minutes. Here is your step-by-step DIY guide.

1. Google Yourself First (The Reality Check)

Before you start, open an incognito browser tab and search your name in quotes, followed by your city (e.g., “John Doe” “Atlanta”).

You’ll likely see sites like FastPeopleSearch, Whitepages, Spokeo, and Radaris pop up on the first page. These are your targets.

2. Setting Up Your Burner Armor (2 Minutes)

To submit opt-out requests, you will often need to provide an email and sometimes a phone number to verify you are who you say you are. Do not use your real ones.

Before starting, create a free burner email account using a secure service like ProtonMail or Tuta. If a site requires a phone number to send a verification code, use a free temporary number service or Google Voice. Keep this burner “identity” active just for opt-out requests to ensure your real accounts stay private.

3. How to Opt Out of the “Big 3” Brokers (15 Minutes)

Below is the step-by-step process to manually remove your records from three of the largest public data aggregators:

Site A: FastPeopleSearch

FastPeopleSearch is one of the most widely used and intrusive free databases online.

  • The Quick Opt-Out: Head directly to fastpeoplesearch.com/removal (do not search on their homepage).
  • Process: Enter your burner email, check the agreement boxes, and solve the CAPTCHA.
  • Removal: Search for your record, click on your profile, and select “Remove My Record”. You will receive a verification link in your burner email. Click it, and your record should disappear within 72 hours.
