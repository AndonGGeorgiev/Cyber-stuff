Domain Scam Checker

Got a suspicious link in a text message or email? Paste the website address below to get a risk estimate based on a live scan of the site and how old the domain is.

How to read the result

  • High risk: do not open the link and do not enter any information.
  • Medium risk: some warning signs were found. Be very careful and check the address letter by letter.
  • Low risk: no major red flags were found, but this does not mean the site is safe. New scam sites appear every day and may not be flagged yet. Never enter passwords or card details through a link you received. Open the official website or app yourself instead.
  • Unknown: the site could not be scanned. It may be offline or blocking scanners.

About the data

This checker is a small tool I built myself. It does not rely on one fixed list of bad websites. Instead, it combines several signals into a single risk score from 0 to 100:

  • A live scan: the domain is scanned with urlscan.io, a free public scanning service. If the domain was scanned very recently, that earlier result is reused.
  • Security verdicts: whether urlscan.io, other security engines or its community have flagged the site as malicious.
  • Brand imitation: whether the page looks like a well-known brand, such as a bank or a courier, while living on a different domain.
  • Redirects: whether the site sends visitors on to a different domain than the one you entered.
  • Domain age: how long ago the domain was registered. Scam sites are often only days or weeks old, so a very new domain adds to the score.

The score is converted into a level: low (under 20), medium (20 to 49) or high (50 and above). The points given to each signal are my own estimates and may be adjusted over time.

Your privacy

Only the domain name is sent for checking. The rest of the link (path, parameters, anything after the domain) is removed in your browser before sending. The scan is submitted to urlscan.io as an unlisted scan, so it does not appear in their public search. Finished results are kept in the checking service's memory for up to an hour so that repeated checks are faster.

Limits

  • This is an automated estimate, not a guarantee. A low score does not prove that a site is safe, because brand-new scam sites often have not been flagged yet.
  • Some sites block scanners or are offline, in which case the result is "unknown".
  • The number of checks is limited per visitor and per day, so you may occasionally be asked to wait.
  • The first check after a quiet period can take up to a minute, because the checking service has to wake up.
